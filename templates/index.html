Speaker setup

  • Calibrate levels Turns volume and bass all the way down so the app can count from zero.
  • Bluetooth pairing Makes the speaker visible to a new phone or computer.
  • Factory reset Erases the speaker's paired devices.
  • Quit app Stops Z407 Control on this Mac.

Factory reset and quit are only available on the Mac running the app.

Good to know
  • The speaker only accepts volume and bass changes while audio is playing.
  • Loudness depends on both the Mac volume and the speaker volume. If it's too quiet, check both.
  • AUX usually sounds better than Bluetooth, which can distort at high volume.
  • Closing this tab doesn't quit the app. It keeps running in the menu bar, where you can reopen or quit it.
  • Mac media keys need Accessibility permission under System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  • Keyboard: ↑ ↓ volume, ← → bass, M mute.
